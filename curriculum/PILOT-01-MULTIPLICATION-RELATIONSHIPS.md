# Pilot Learning Chain 01 · Multiplication Relationships

**Release status:** Ready for family test  
**Interface language:** English  
**Purpose:** Validate the complete check → learn → independent application → delayed check loop before expanding production.

## Why this chain comes first

Both family learners can work on the same mathematical structure at different depths. The younger learner can connect equal groups to multiplication. The older learner can expose whether fast calculation is supported by distributive reasoning, fact-family understanding, and transfer to an unfamiliar problem.

## Five missions

| Mission | Purpose | Main evidence | TEKS focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Find Your Starting Point | Select useful depth without treating reported grade level as mastery | Concept starting evidence | 2.6A, 3.4D–E, 4.4C |
| 2. Build Equal Groups | Connect rows, groups, repeated addition, and multiplication | Concept and procedure | 2.6A, 3.4D–E |
| 3. Split Without Changing the Total | See and explain the distributive relationship | Concept, procedure, explanation | 3.4E, 4.4B–C |
| 4. Multiply and Divide the Same Array | Connect inverse operations and unknowns | Procedure and explanation | 2.6B, 3.4J, 3.5D |
| 5. Fresh Garden Challenge | Transfer after time has passed | Application and explanation | 3.4K, 3.5B, 4.4H, 5.4B |

## Three depths

- **Explorer:** small equal groups and visible counting.
- **Strategist:** multiplication/division fact families and two-part splits.
- **Architect:** larger arrays, distributive reasoning, missing quantities, and multi-step transfer.

The names do not correspond to a school grade. Mission 1 recommends a depth; the parent can change it.

## Evidence rules for this pilot

- First-try success without a hint can support independent evidence.
- Success after a hint or correction records learning progress, not independent mastery.
- Accidental taps can be retried with “That was an accidental tap” before the answer is revealed.
- Reading difficulty should be handled by reading the prompt aloud; it is not automatically recorded as a mathematics error.
- Mission 5 is due after a delay. Parent preview mode can inspect it immediately, but preview results do not count as delayed evidence.
- Passing Mission 4 marks relevant skills “temporarily passed — waiting for a fresh check.” The learner may continue other learning while waiting.
- Family validation requires recording three observations: where help was needed, whether the child could explain, and whether the child wanted to continue.

## What this pilot does not claim

- It does not diagnose a whole grade.
- It does not generate CogAT, Iowa, or placement scores.
- It does not prove long-term mastery or general educational effectiveness.
- It does not mark all linked TEKS expectations as covered until content review and family evidence are recorded.
