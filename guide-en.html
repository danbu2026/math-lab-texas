Build understanding through play

Choose one small activity. Explore the model, try without it, then explain your thinking. Grade ranges describe the content, not who is allowed to play.

Start here

  1. Choose Explorer A or B. Their practice and notes are saved separately.
  2. Try Number Workshop: tap a gold bead or drag it left to make ten. Ask what changed and what stayed the same.
  3. In Block Builder, drag with a finger or mouse to turn the model. Arrow keys and view buttons work too.
  4. Choose “Try it” for three practice questions. A hint or another attempt is recorded as “with help.” Hints guide thinking; an explanation follows a correct answer.
  5. Try new numbers another day. One successful session does not prove lasting mastery.

What is included?

18 original activities on selected elementary topics. This is a supplementary starter, not a complete curriculum, placement test, or official exam simulator.

Each lab has one fixed “Think deeper” challenge. Repeating it practices the same problem; it is not a new test of transfer. Ordinary practice can change numbers, but the range of question types is limited.

Texas curriculum and assessments

The activities reference broad K–5 mathematics themes. They have not been audited against every Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills requirement. Some spatial and logic activities are enrichment.

Not fully covered: place value, standard written algorithms, time, money, financial literacy, data displays, measurement units, coordinates, all fraction and decimal operations, or all shape classifications. Mixed practice is not an official STAAR test and does not predict scores.

Language, records and offline use

Use the English / 中文 button to switch the whole app. The two languages share the same questions and learning record. Notes stay exactly as the learner wrote them. Your selected language is saved on this device. Direct links can choose a language with ?lang=en or ?lang=zh.

Completed answers and notes are saved locally. An unfinished practice round starts again after refresh. Clearing browser data removes records. The clear-record button asks for confirmation and affects only the selected learner.

After the first online visit, core files are cached for offline use in the same browser. Browsers may clear caches. Private hosting may need an internet connection for first access or renewed sign-in.

iPad / iPhone: open in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer, use your browser’s Install or Add to Home Screen option when available.

For the local copy, run the project’s start shortcut and open http://localhost:4185/. Do not open index.html directly; modules and offline files need a local server.

What does success mean?

A working app is not proof of educational effectiveness. This version has not undergone a child study, teacher certification, or test-score study. Look for a child who can explain why, solve without the model, and use the idea in a different situation—not just get an answer right.
