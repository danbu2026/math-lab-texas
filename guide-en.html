Use the system in small, useful steps

Little Math Lab is being built as a complete K–5 learning system for children who may be ahead in some areas and still have hidden gaps in others. The goal is deeper understanding, independent application, clear explanation, and flexible reasoning.

What is ready now?

  • Complete System Blueprint: all 287 official K–5 TEKS student expectations, the production plan for every ability, personal-path rules, evidence rules, parent support, rewards, and honest build status.
  • Pilot Path 01: five connected missions about multiplication relationships at three depths.
  • 18 Math Prototypes: manipulatives and practice ideas that can later become parts of complete learning chains.

Gray items are planned and not published. Gold items belong to the first pilot. The framework is visible before all lessons are complete so the family can see what exists, what is missing, and what comes next.

Start here

  1. Open the Complete System Map and review the Big Picture.
  2. Use K–5 Curriculum to inspect any grade, strand, or official expectation.
  3. Open Pilot Path 01, choose Ella or Mila, and select the best learning depth.
  4. Let the child think before giving a hint. Record accidental taps instead of treating them as learning errors.
  5. Return for the fresh check later. One correct answer today is not enough evidence of stable learning.

How the finished system will work

Every content ability receives four connected session types: Check, Learn, Apply, and Fresh Check. Mathematical process standards are woven into these sessions. The daily selector will choose one useful ten-minute mission based on prerequisite gaps, a due fresh check, readiness for a new idea, or a transfer opportunity.

Evidence stays separate across concept, procedure, application, and explanation or transfer. A child can skip stable abilities, revisit one specific prerequisite, and continue into deeper reasoning without repeating an entire grade.

Standards and assessment context

TEKS and Carroll ISD context define the mathematics scope and sequence. Independent reading, calculation, data, and multi-step application support the kinds of achievement measured by Iowa/ITBS. Quantitative, visual, spatial, and language reasoning support the kinds of thinking organized by CogAT. This project does not copy or claim to reproduce any protected test.

  • Texas K–5 Mathematics TEKS
  • Carroll ISD Mathematics
  • Carroll ISD Testing for GT Math Placement

Records, offline use, and iPad

Current records stay in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them. After the first online visit, core pages are cached for offline use, although a browser can later clear its cache.

On iPad, open the live site in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, and tap Add. A physical iPad/Safari family test is still required before we call the experience validated.

Current limits

Most formal K–5 sessions are not written yet. The daily selector, complete prerequisite graph, full parent dashboard, accounts, cross-device sync, membership, and payment system are future work. The product has not completed a formal child study or test-score study.
