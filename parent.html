What is Little Math Lab?

A growing K–5 learning system for short, meaningful practice. Children solve, explain, test, and revisit ideas instead of racing through repetitive worksheets.

10 useful minutes
🎯

The purpose

Build strong mathematical understanding, reasoning, spatial thinking, and problem-solving habits that support school learning and future study.

🧠

How learning works

Check what the child knows, teach one idea, apply it in a new situation, then use a later Fresh Check to see what truly stayed.

🧭

Levels, not age labels

Children move by demonstrated readiness. A Level is a challenge to master, not a limit based on age or school grade.

⭐

Motivation with meaning

Completed released missions earn credits toward family-chosen rewards. There are no rankings, streak penalties, or pressure to stay online.

HOW YOUR CHILD USES IT

One mission at a time

  1. 1Choose Ella or Mila. Each learner keeps a separate path and record.
  2. 2Open Today. Start the next available ten-minute mission.
  3. 3Let the child think first. Hints help, but independent work is recorded separately.
  4. 4Ask for an explanation. “How do you know?” matters as much as the final answer.
  5. 5Stop when the mission is done. Short, focused use is the design—not endless screen time.

THE PARENT’S ROLE

Observe more than you teach

  • Give the child time before offering help.
  • Ask what changed, what stayed the same, and why.
  • Use the Progress page to see completed work.
  • Choose rewards together and keep them meaningful.
  • Tell us when instructions confuse the child—that is product evidence, not failure.
